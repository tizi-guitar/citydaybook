CITY DAYBOOK

A whole day in 96 seconds.

City Daybook watches public webcams across Europe frame by frame, day and night, and every morning turns each camera's day into a single time-lapse: one frame every 30 seconds, played back so that one second of video stands for a quarter of an hour of real time.

The light moves, the weather turns, the traffic thins out at four in the morning and comes back at seven. One new day from each camera, every day.

Where to watch

youtube.com/@citydaybook

Get in touch

If one of these cameras is yours, or you have a question about a video, write to citydaybook@yahoo.com.

What this software is

The channel is fed by a small personal automation running on its owner's server. It captures the frames, assembles the videos, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It has exactly one user - the person who runs it - and it collects nothing from anyone else.

Credits

Every video credits the webcam it comes from, with a link to its live stream, and the music it carries. The images belong to the people who run those cameras; what is made here is the day, compressed.
