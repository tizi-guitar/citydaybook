# City Daybook

The public pages of the [City Daybook](https://www.youtube.com/@citydaybook)
YouTube channel: a channel that turns a whole day of a public webcam into 96
seconds of time-lapse, one city at a time.

This repository holds two static pages and nothing else - the home page and the
privacy policy of the small personal automation that feeds the channel. The
software itself lives elsewhere and is not published here.

- `index.html` - what the channel is
- `privacy.html` - what the uploader accesses, and what it does not
