Privacy Policy

City Daybook uploader · last updated 16 September 2026

City Daybook is a personal, single-user automation. It records public webcam frames, assembles them into daily time-lapse videos, and publishes those videos to one YouTube channel: the one owned by the person running the software. There are no other users, no accounts to create, and no visitors whose data could be collected.

What data the application accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the application obtains an OAuth authorization for the owner's own Google account, limited to these scopes:

  • youtube.upload - to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel;
  • youtube - to set the visibility, titles, descriptions and playlists of those same videos, and the channel's own branding;
  • youtube.readonly - to check whether a video has already been uploaded, so that nothing is uploaded twice.

No other Google product is accessed. No contacts, mail, files, calendar or profile information are read.

What is stored, and where

The OAuth tokens are stored as a single file on the owner's own private server, readable only by the owner's account. They never leave that machine, are never sent to any third party, and are used solely to talk to Google's own API endpoints. They can be revoked by the owner at any time, which immediately and permanently stops the application from working.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone.
  • No data is used for advertising, profiling, or audience measurement.
  • No human reads the data, and it is not used to train any model.
  • No analytics, cookies or trackers are used by this software or by the pages you are reading.

Limited use

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing consent

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access deletes the application's ability to reach any Google data; the stored token becomes useless and can be deleted from the server.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the About page of the channel at youtube.com/@citydaybook.
